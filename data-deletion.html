Data deletion

Applies to the WAAD website and the WAAD Publisher tool

WAAD Publisher does not keep accounts or personal data for members of the public. If one of your public posts appeared in our short-lived reply shortlist, it is removed automatically within 30 days.

How to request deletion

  1. Email worldasadataset@proton.me with the subject "Data deletion" and the username or post link concerned.
  2. We delete any matching records within 30 days and confirm by reply.

If you want a reply WAAD posted under your post removed, say so in the same email and we will delete it.
