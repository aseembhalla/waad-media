Privacy policy

Effective 8 November 2026 · Applies to the WAAD website and the WAAD Publisher tool

Who we are

WAAD (World As A Dataset) is a data-infographics publisher operated by Aseem Bhalla. WAAD Publisher is an internal tool that publishes WAAD's own content to WAAD's own social media accounts.

What data we collect

WAAD Publisher does not collect, store or process personal data about members of the public. It connects only to accounts owned by WAAD (Instagram, Facebook Page, Pinterest, YouTube and X) using access permissions granted by WAAD itself through each platform's official authorization flow.

For those WAAD-owned accounts, the tool stores only the access credentials needed to publish, kept encrypted in a private repository, and basic publishing records (what was posted, where and when). It may read public performance statistics of WAAD's own posts (for example reach or saves) to decide which topics to publish next.

What we do not do

  • We do not read, collect or store data about other users, followers or their content.
  • We do not sell or share data with third parties.
  • We do not use cookies or analytics on this website.

Use of Google and YouTube data

WAAD Publisher uses the YouTube Data API solely to upload WAAD's own videos to WAAD's own YouTube channel. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. By using YouTube features you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's processing is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.

Retention and deletion

Access credentials are deleted when a platform connection is removed. Publishing records are kept for as long as WAAD operates. To request deletion of any data relating to you, contact us via Instagram @worldasadataset.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change.
